## Project Overview
This project examines various variables and their relationships using R. The analysis focuses on identifying patterns in sleep disorders, sleep quality, and sleep duration.

## Dataset
[Sleep Health and Lifestyle Dataset - Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/uom190346a/sleep-health-and-lifestyle-dataset)

## Research Questions and Methods
**Main Question**
How are age, stress levels, and physical activity levels related to sleep disorders, quality, and duration? 

**Subquestion 1**
How does the presence of sleep disorders vary across age groups? 
- Frequency and relative frequency tables
  
- 100% stacked bar chart

- Chi-square test of independence

**Subquestion 2**
Does sleep quality differ across age groups? 
- Mean and standard deviation

- Bar chart comparing average sleep quality

- One-way ANOVA

**Subquestion 3**
How is stress level related to sleep quality? 
- Pearson correlation coefficient

- Scatterplot with fitted regression line

- Pearson correlation hypothesis test

**Subquestion 4**
How does sleep duration vary across stress level groups? 
- Mean and median

- Bar chart comparing average sleep duration

- One-way ANOVA

**Subquestion 5**
How is physical activity level related to sleep duration? 
- Pearson correlation coefficient

- Scatterplot with fitted regression line

- Pearson correlation hypothesis test
